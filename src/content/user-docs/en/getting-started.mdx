# Get started with Tracky

Tracky brings balances, transactions, Plan assignments, due dates, and credit into a forward-looking view. The Dashboard explains your current position; Plan allocates the money already in your accounts; Cash Flow answers “what happens to my cash when the next commitments arrive?”.

<Callout title="First rule">
  Always verify the currency, balance, and last update before making a decision. Projections depend on data stored in
  the app, not on a guaranteed real-time bank balance.
</Callout>

## Recommended setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your bank">
    Open **Settings → Bank Connections**, start a connection, and complete consent at your bank. Tracky imports the
    accounts, balances, and transactions the provider makes available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review accounts">
    Add useful aliases, hide items you do not want in general totals, and check sync health and consent expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review transactions">
    Open **All Accounts**, or select one account from the sidebar to open its ledger. Correct categories and
    classifications and confirm subscription and transfer suggestions. These choices feed Plan, Reports, and Cash Flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the cash-flow view">
    Add known due items, income, transfers, and money boxes. Set a Cash Flow cycle that matches your salary or review
    period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Navigation

The primary destinations are **Dashboard**, **Plan**, **Reports**, **All Accounts**, and **Cash Flow**. **Forecast**, **Goals**, and **Subscriptions** are in Tools. Your accounts appear below in **Cash**, **Credit**, and **Loans** groups; selecting an account opens its own ledger. **Settings** is at the bottom, while **Bank Connections** is also available with the account controls below the account list. The command bar provides fast navigation and actions, and **Get Help** searches these guides.

## Language and amounts

Change language from the command bar. Money is stored as integer minor units with a currency code: `EUR 12.34` is `1234` cents. Totals in different currencies remain separate and are never converted implicitly.

## Your first useful check

Compare Tracky's preferred balance with the available or booked figure supplied by the bank, then inspect recent transactions, recurring payments, this month's Ready to Assign and Available amounts, and the first projected negative date. If an account or card is unavailable from the provider, create a manual account.
