# Project your long-term finances

Open **Forecast** to model how your net worth, account balances, income, and expenses may change over the coming years. Every scenario starts from live Tracky balances and applies the assumptions and life events you choose. A forecast is a planning estimate, not a guarantee of future returns.

<Callout title="Forecast is a Pro feature">
  Forecast scenarios require the Pro plan. Free accounts see an upgrade prompt instead of the setup flow and projection.
</Callout>

## Complete the initial setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your age and retirement milestone">
    Enter your birth year and choose a planned retirement age on the timeline. Tracky uses them to label yearly checkpoints and choose a sensible end age for the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review accounts">
    Include or exclude each detected asset account and adjust its expected annual growth. Active liabilities with complete rate and payment data are added automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review income sources">
    Keep, rename, or remove each source detected from recent complete months, and add other recurring monthly income when needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the expense baseline">
    Confirm or override the detected monthly living-expense baseline. This changes the forecast assumption only; it does not edit transaction data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Accounts in another currency are not converted. Credit facilities missing a usable currency, interest rate, or monthly payment are excluded, and Forecast shows a notice when data could not be projected.

## Read the projection

The summary cards show net worth at the end of the current year, net worth at retirement, planned retirement age, and net worth at the end of the plan. When comparing scenarios, monetary cards also show the signed difference from the comparison scenario.

The chart follows net worth through each yearly checkpoint. Hover a point to see the calendar year, age, net worth, annual income, annual expenses, and life events in that year. Event dots and vertical reference lines mark milestones; a red reference line marks the projected depletion year when one exists.

Use the value toggle to switch between:

- **Today's euros**, which removes cumulative inflation and makes purchasing power comparable across years;
- **Future euros**, which shows nominal amounts expected in each future year.

The same choice applies to the summary, chart tooltip, and year-by-year tables.

## Add and manage life events

Open the **Events** tab below the chart, then choose **Add life event**. Forecast supports:

- retirement and pension income;
- buying a home with cash or a mortgage;
- having a child;
- a career break or a new job;
- other one-time or recurring income and expenses;
- a custom end-of-plan age.

Money fields use today's major currency units. You can edit an event, temporarily disable it without deleting it, or delete it after confirmation. Enabled events appear on the chart and affect the projection. If an older saved event is no longer valid—for example, its year is now in the past—Forecast keeps the page usable and lists the reason in an alert and on the event row.

## Create and compare scenarios

The scenario bar switches between saved plans. Each scenario can have its own name, icon, color, assumptions, income sources, account settings, and life events.

Choose **New scenario** to either:

- duplicate the current scenario, including its assumptions and events; or
- create a fresh scenario from current account balances and newly detected income and expense baselines.

Open scenario settings to rename or style the current scenario, move it up or down, duplicate it, or delete it after confirmation.

To compare plans, choose exactly one other scenario from **Compare with…**. Forecast overlays its net-worth line in a second style, adds a legend, and shows signed deltas in the monetary summary cards. The active and comparison scenario are kept in the page URL, so the selected view survives navigation and can be bookmarked. Remove the **vs _scenario_** pill to leave comparison mode.

## Inspect yearly accounts, cash flow, and events

The **Accounts** table keeps account names fixed while you scroll across every projected year. Asset balances are positive, liabilities are negative, and **Extra savings** contains surplus cash not allocated to another account. Select the settings button on an account row to include or exclude it, change growth or yearly contributions, or override liability rate and payment assumptions.

The **Cash Flow** tab lists annual income, expenses, and savings. The first year may cover only the remaining months of the current calendar year; later rows normally cover a full year.

The **Events** tab lists every saved life event for the current scenario and provides enable, edit, and delete controls.

## Edit assumptions or start over

Choose **Edit assumptions** to update inflation, plan end age, monthly living expenses and their change mode, extra-savings growth, capital-gains tax, and recurring income sources.

Changes save assumptions only. Current account balances and live credit-facility values continue to refresh from Tracky, and every affected projection recalculates reactively.

Use **Reset forecast** at the bottom of the assumptions panel only when you want to delete every scenario and return to guided onboarding. The action requires confirmation.
