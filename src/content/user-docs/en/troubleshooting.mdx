# Troubleshooting

## Balance differs from the bank

Check latest snapshot date, sync status, booked versus available balance, and transactions the bank has not published yet. For a manual account, review direction and amount; editing or deleting adjusts balance automatically.

## Net worth looks too low

Check negative CARD balances, independent financing, and currency. Cards are not cash. If one purchase appears in several forms, confirm statement and payment are transfer/internal and the facility is linked to the correct CARD.

## Cash Flow shows an expense twice

Link the real transaction to the planned occurrence or mark that occurrence paid. Avoid a duplicate planned expense for a subscription. For cards, purchase is spending while statement settlement is a transfer.

## An item is unassigned

Assign an account to the expense or subscription. For a card statement, configure the facility's settlement account. Until then the commitment is visible but cannot reduce one specific account series.

## A transfer is not recognized

Check distinct accounts, opposite directions, matching currency, at most seven days apart, and amounts within tolerance. Confirm a candidate in Review. A leg already matched or classified `internal` cannot be reused.

## A manual transaction cannot be edited or deleted

First unlink its transfer match. If it is used by Cash Flow, an installment, statement, plan, money box, or subscription, remove or correct that relationship; Tracky blocks deletion to prevent dangling references.

## Card usage cannot be edited

That is expected for a CARD-linked facility: usage is `max(-balance, 0)`. Correct the card movements. Manual usage belongs only to the legacy flow without CARD.

## Analyst does not respond

Check message limits, wait for a running response, or choose another model. A failed tool is not a successful change without the displayed confirmation. For Telegram, regenerate an expired code and send it only in the private chat.

## Bank consent expired

Open Settings → Bank Connections, renew consent, and complete the bank flow again. History remains, and sync resumes when the connection becomes active.
