# Manual transactions and transfers

Open **All Accounts**, or the ledger for one account, and choose **Add transaction**. The new movement is entered directly in a row aligned with the ledger columns.

A manual account accepts booked movements dated today or earlier and scheduled movements dated in the future. A bank-linked account accepts only scheduled manual entries: the bank remains authoritative for booked movements and balances.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the account and date">
    On All Accounts, select the account. A future date creates a scheduled movement; on a bank-linked account the date
    starts at tomorrow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Enter the payee when useful, a required description, an optional category, and an optional memo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter one direction">
    Use **Outflow** for money leaving or **Inflow** for money arriving. Enter a positive value in the account currency;
    the two fields are mutually exclusive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue or close">
    Choose **Save and add another** to keep the same account and date for the next row, or **Save** to close the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduled entries

A future one-off movement appears in the Scheduled band above booked rows. It does not move the current balance or enter spending and Reports before its date. On a manual account, Tracky books it when due. On a linked account, reconcile it with the bank's own row after that row arrives.

## Automatic transfer matching

On creation, Tracky searches another account for an opposite leg with the same currency and a date within seven days before or after. Amount tolerance is `min(max(2% of amount, 300 minor units), 1000 minor units)`.

Confidence starts at `0.90` for an exact amount, `0.82` for a difference up to 300 minor units, and `0.72` for other allowed differences, minus `0.02` per day apart, with a `0.60` floor. High-confidence matches are automatic; others appear in Review.

## Update and delete

Open an existing manual movement to edit it. Moving a booked manual row into the future turns it into scheduled and removes its previous balance effect; moving it back to today or earlier books it again.

Unlink a confirmed transfer before editing or deleting one of its legs. Deletion is blocked when the transaction is linked to a planned payment, installment, card statement, installment plan, money-box contribution, subscription, or another confirmed relationship. Unconfirmed candidates are removed with it, and the balance is restored.

<Callout title="Card settlement">
  Record the imported Outflow on the current account and an Inflow on the manual CARD account. Matching them excludes
  the transfer from spending and can settle the scheduled statement.
</Callout>
