# Mortgages and loans

A mortgage, car loan, or personal loan is added like an account. Choose **Add Account** below the sidebar account list, select the loan type, and enter the terms the lender uses: current balance, interest rate, required monthly payment, any escrow or fees, the cash account debited, and the first payment date.

Loans appear in their own sidebar group. Selecting one opens its page instead of a transaction ledger.

## What the loan page shows

The page brings together:

- the outstanding balance;
- the interest rate and required installment;
- the estimated or contractual payoff date;
- a chart of the remaining balance falling over time;
- the Plan category that funds the installment;
- payments already recorded against the loan.

Use **Update Balance** when the lender reports a new outstanding balance. Use **Edit** to correct the terms or Plan pairing.

## Original principal

**Original principal** is the amount you borrowed at the start. It is optional and affects only the progress shown on the loan page: when you provide it, Tracky can display how much principal has already been repaid and the percentage of the original loan that represents.

Leave it empty if you do not know it. The current balance, payment schedule, and amount owed do not change. The original principal cannot be lower than the current outstanding balance.

## Payoff date from the contract

If the contract states the date of the last installment, enter it as **Payoff date**. Without that date, Tracky estimates the end from today's balance, interest rate, and installment.

That estimate can be years early for an Italian variable-rate mortgage. Some lenders keep the capital repayment timetable fixed and change the installment when the rate changes. In that case today's rate and installment do not reveal the real end of the contract. Entering the contractual payoff date makes the displayed date, chart, and planned installments follow the contract instead.

## Pair the loan with a Plan category

You can select one of your ordinary Plan categories while creating or editing the loan. That category then carries the loan's monthly need and due date. Tracky does not create a second Instalments row beside it, so the same payment is not requested twice.

The category keeps its name, group, target, assignments, and category mappings. If you remove the pairing, the category stays where it is and an unpaired loan receives its own generated installment row.

<Callout title="Use the lender's figures">
  Tracky projects the terms you enter. It does not replace the contract or the lender's outstanding-balance statement.
  Update the balance and terms when the lender changes them.
</Callout>
