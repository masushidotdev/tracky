# Settings, plan, and privacy

Open **Settings** to review account-level preferences and privacy controls. Changes here do not move money or authorize bank payments.

## How Settings is organised

Settings opens on **General** and carries four more pages across the top:

- **Bank connections** - the banks you have linked, their status, and the button
  that adds another. Choosing it opens a window where you pick your bank and are
  sent to it to authenticate; nothing is connected until you come back. If the
  provider is not configured on this instance, the button is disabled.
- **Accounts** - every account, linked or manual: rename, hide, turn sync off,
  correct a manual balance.
- **Credit** - credit cards, their statement cycles, and the loans and instalment
  contracts you track.
- **Import history** - the CSV imports you have run and what each one produced.

The account list in the sidebar is for reading your money; these pages are for
administering it. Adding an account is still one click from the sidebar.

## Profile

The Profile card shows the name, email address, and locale associated with your signed-in profile. It is read-only: there are no profile-editing controls on this page.

## Free and Pro plans

The Plan card shows your current tier and daily Analyst allowance:

- **Free:** 20 Analyst messages per day.
- **Pro:** 100 Analyst messages per day and access to long-term FIRE projections.

Connected and manual accounts, [CSV import](/app/docs/csv-import), Dashboard and Safe to spend, Cash Flow and bill reminders, and money boxes remain available on both tiers.

<Callout title="Billing is not available yet">
  Settings reports your current access, but you cannot buy, upgrade, downgrade, or manage billing in Tracky yet. The
  Pro action is marked as coming soon.
</Callout>

## Usage analytics

Tracky collects product analytics linked to your account to plan improvements:
pages visited and features used. Nothing is collected until you press **Accept**
on the banner; pressing **Reject** keeps everything local and nothing is sent.

Events are pseudonymous: they carry your internal account id but never your
name, email, or contact details. What is never sent: amounts, balances, notes,
account names, bank names, message text, or file contents. Events carry only
counters such as which page was viewed or which feature was used.

The **Usage analytics** card in Settings shows the current choice and lets you
change it anytime: enabling starts collection, disabling stops it immediately.
Session recordings (screen captures for usability) follow the same choice and
mask every input field plus balances and amounts.

Deleting your account stops future account-linked usage events. The account erasure
process removes Tracky records and closes your sign-in account; any previously
collected product analytics follow their separate retention process.

## Notification preferences

**Bill reminder lead days** tell Tracky how far before a planned expense occurrence to create a reminder. The default is **3 days**; `0` means the due date itself. You can select up to four values from 0 to 30. With multiple values, Tracky uses the smallest selected threshold reached by the occurrence, so `7` and `3` can produce reminders as each window is reached.

In-app bill reminders need no delivery opt-in when at least one lead day is selected. **Email** and **Telegram** delivery are both off by default. Their switches currently apply to bill reminders, upcoming subscription or installment payments, and low projected-balance warnings. Telegram delivery also requires a verified link.

See [Analyst, notifications, and automations](/app/docs/analyst-notifications-automations) for recurrence, paid-occurrence, and delivery details.

## Telegram link

Generate the one-time `/link` command, then send it privately to the configured bot. It expires after ten minutes and can be used once. Never share the command. You can unlink the current chat from the same card.

## Analyst memories

Memories are facts, preferences, or goals the Analyst can save—with your approval—and retrieve in later relevant conversations. The list shows the stored text and its kind.

Use the trash action and confirm to delete one memory. Deletion removes that saved item from future memory retrieval; it does not delete the conversation where it originated.

## Export your data

Request a version 3 JSON export to receive the Tracky records associated with your user:

- accounts and balance snapshots; transactions, categories, and category rules;
- plans, groups, buckets, category mappings, assignments, targets, subscriptions, planned expenses and occurrence payments;
- money boxes and contributions; credit facilities and planned transfers;
- notifications, Analyst memory text and kinds, and user settings.

This includes balances and transactions maintained through [manual accounts](/app/docs/manual-accounts) or [CSV import](/app/docs/csv-import). Analyst memory embeddings are not included.

Only one export can be queued or running at a time. After an export completes, wait 24 hours before requesting another from the Data export card. Its download link expires seven days after the request. The deletion dialog can request an export without the daily limit, or offer a recent completed export, so you can save a copy before erasure.

## Delete your account

In **Danger zone**, choose **Delete account**. You can prepare an optional export and download it before confirming. If you request an export in the dialog, wait until it is ready and download it before continuing. Type the email address shown in the dialog to enable **Delete everything**.

Confirmation starts immediate, irreversible erasure. Tracky disconnects linked banks, removes your exports, financial records, plans, forecasts, notifications, Analyst memories and settings in batches, and closes the sign-in account last. The progress page updates while this runs; you can close the browser and the server continues. When finished, Tracky signs you out and returns you to the homepage. There is no grace period or undo.

<Callout title="What remains">
  Tracky keeps a SHA-256 hash of the account identifier and the deletion date for up to 12 months as a deletion record. It contains no email, name, financial records, or export file. Bank-consent revocation is attempted during erasure and retried if a provider is unavailable; you can also revoke access directly at your bank. Previously sent email delivery records may remain until the email provider's retention cleanup. Previously collected usage analytics follow the separate retention process described above.
</Callout>
