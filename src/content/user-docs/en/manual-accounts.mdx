# Manual cash, card, and asset accounts

A manual account covers cash, a card, an investment, or another asset that the bank integration does not expose. Choose `CACC`, `SVGS`, `CARD`, `INVS`, or `ASST`; currency must be a three-letter code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    From **Accounts → Add manual account**, enter name, type, currency, and optional alias. Tracky creates an active
    account with sync disabled and an opening zero-balance snapshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the opening position">
    For checking, savings, or card accounts, record an `internal` manual movement so the opening amount is not treated
    as income or spending. For an investment or other asset, open its actions and choose **Update balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain the balance">
    Record movements for cash and cards. For investments and other assets, record a new dated balance whenever their
    value changes materially.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cash and card types

**CACC** represents a checking account and **SVGS** a savings account. Both count as cash in Dashboard, Safe to spend, and cashflow: `CRDT` increases the balance and `DBIT` decreases it.

**CARD** represents the card ledger. Purchase debits normally take the balance below zero; refunds and settlement credits move it back toward zero. A negative CARD balance is debt, not cash.

<CalculationBreakdown title="Manual balance">
  New balance = previous balance + transaction effect.

`effect(CRDT) = +amount`; `effect(DBIT) = −amount`. Updating applies the difference between new and old effect; deletion applies the reverse effect.

**CARD example:** opening `0`, purchase `DBIT 8000`, refund `CRDT 2000` → balance `-6000` minor units.

</CalculationBreakdown>

## Investment and other-asset types

**INVS** represents investments and **ASST** another asset. They appear in the **Assets** group on Accounts. Use the account actions menu → **Update balance**, enter the value and reference date, then save. Each update appends a balance snapshot; Tracky uses the newest one rather than deriving value from purchases or sales.

Their visible balances enter the Dashboard net-worth breakdown as `cash + assets − debts`. They never enter Safe to spend or cashflow, because they are not spendable cash. Investment and other-asset balances also seed the Analyst's starting invested balance for [Pro FIRE projections](/app/docs/analyst-notifications-automations#fire-and-retirement-projections).

## Operational limits

Manual accounts have no sync health or refresh controls. A movement needs a positive amount, matching currency, description, and `YYYY-MM-DD` date. [CSV import](/app/docs/csv-import) accepts active manual `CACC` and `CARD` accounts, not savings or asset types. Never use a fake positive CARD balance as its limit: the limit belongs to the linked credit facility.

To download manual account records and their balance snapshots, request the JSON export from [Settings](/app/docs/settings-and-privacy#export-your-data).
