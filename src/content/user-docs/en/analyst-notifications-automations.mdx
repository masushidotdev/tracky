# Analyst, notifications, and automations

The AI Analyst section is available only when enabled in this environment. Otherwise, its sidebar and command-palette entries are hidden, and opening `/app/analyst` shows “Feature disabled” and “The AI Analyst is not enabled in this environment.” This does not stop backend automations or Telegram.

When enabled, Analyst can read authorized Tracky data, explain patterns, simulate what-if scenarios, and present charts or tables. You can manage multiple conversations and choose an available model and reasoning level.

## Reads and changes

Read tools cover accounts, transactions, spending, Plan progress, cashflow, money boxes, credit, subscriptions, and planned items. Sensitive writes require a preview and your approval: Plan assignments or targets, money boxes, planned items, memory, and bulk recategorization. Denial leaves data unchanged.

<Callout title="Human control">
  Always check amount, currency, account, category, date, and recurrence. Analyst is an exploration aid, not financial
  advice or a replacement for contract data.
</Callout>

## FIRE and retirement projections

Ask the Analyst in ordinary language, for example: “When could I reach FIRE?” or “Project retirement in 20 years with 3% inflation.” The Pro-only long-term projection starts from your tracked cash, investment and other-asset balances, and the next three months of Cash Flow inflows and outflows.

Unless you override them in chat, it uses a 30-year horizon, 2% annual expense inflation, 5% annual investment returns, and a 4% safe withdrawal rate. The withdrawal rate derives the default FIRE target from annual expenses. You can replace these assumptions, starting balances, monthly income or expenses, target, retirement date, and one-off events in your request.

Read the assumptions in every result and compare scenarios: projections are nominal estimates, not guarantees. Free accounts cannot run this tool; [Settings](/app/docs/settings-and-privacy) shows your tier and the current plan limits.

## Telegram

Generate a one-time command from [Settings](/app/docs/settings-and-privacy) and send it privately to the configured bot. It expires after ten minutes and works once. Never share it. You can unlink Telegram; requests that need approval continue to state that explicitly.

## Bill reminders

Bill reminders come from planned expenses with a `planned` or `funding` status. Tracky checks the next occurrence—including the next date of a recurring bill—and skips an occurrence already marked paid.

The default lead time is three days. In Settings you can choose up to four thresholds from 0 to 30 days; `0` means the due date. At each evaluation, Tracky uses the smallest selected threshold the occurrence has reached. With `7` and `3`, a bill can therefore create one reminder in the seven-day window and another when it reaches three days. Dedupe prevents repeats for the same occurrence and threshold.

When at least one lead day is configured, the reminder is created in the in-app inbox regardless of external delivery switches. Email and Telegram are separate opt-ins, both off by default; Telegram also has to be linked. Manage these choices in [Settings](/app/docs/settings-and-privacy).

## Financial notifications

Every six hours Tracky evaluates:

- visible, non-snoozed Plan buckets at least 90% funded-and-spent (**warning**) or in the red (**critical**);
- active subscriptions and installments due within seven days;
- the first projected negative cashflow date;
- failed sync or consent requiring renewal.

Dedupe keys prevent the same event from multiplying on every evaluation. Read or dismiss a notification in the UI, then fix the underlying source data.

External delivery currently covers bill reminders, upcoming subscription or installment payments, and low projected-balance warnings. Other notification types remain in the in-app inbox.

## Backend automations

- account sync and notification evaluation: every 6 hours;
- automatic closing of due card cycles: daily;
- Analyst health scores and anomaly detection: daily;
- monthly report: day 2; subscription review: day 3;
- watchdog and cleanup recover interrupted jobs and Telegram updates.

These automations never authorize a bank payment. They update imports, analysis, notifications, and internal state.
